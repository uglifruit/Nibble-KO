NIBBLE-KO Setup

Connecting

  • On the card: hold the switch Down and press B + D together
  • All four pads glow slowly — the card is now on USB and silent
  • Click Connect USB and allow MIDI access when the browser asks

Assigning sounds

  • Every gesture can play any sound: its own synth voice, a built-in sample, or one of yours
  • Changes are heard immediately — no reboot, so you can audition while you pick
  • Click Save Kit to Card to keep them; without that a power cycle undoes them
  • One sample can be played by several voices at once, and costs the space of one

The sample library

  • On the Samples tab, click Add Samples… and pick one or more WAVs — any rate or channel count
  • They are converted to 8-bit mono 48kHz, trimmed, loudness-matched and dithered, exactly as tools/importwav.py does for the built-in ones
  • Click Upload Staged. The card goes silent, writes flash, and restarts
  • Uploads append, so adding one sample leaves the rest alone. Deleting frees the slot but not the bytes — only Erase All reclaims space

Getting back to playing

  • An upload reboots the card by itself
  • Otherwise just power-cycle it — the saved calibration survives, so it comes straight back up playable
